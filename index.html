Georgia Accountability

CCRPI — What Every AP Needs to Know

📊 CCRPI = College & Career Ready Performance Index. Georgia's school accountability system. Griffin High's CCRPI score determines state ratings, interventions, and support. Every metric on this dashboard directly feeds into CCRPI.
📈 Achievement Component 40% of Score
The largest CCRPI component. Measures the percentage of students scoring at Levels 3 (Proficient) and 4 (Distinguished) on GMAS EOC assessments.

AP Connection: Your walkthroughs, unit assessment data, and % failing 1+ course all directly impact this score. When teachers are being observed regularly with feedback, and when intervention happens for failing students, achievement moves.

Key Subjects: Algebra I, American Lit/Comp, Biology, U.S. History
📉 Progress Component 40% of Score
Measures student growth from one year to the next — individual students' trajectories, not just grade-level averages. Students can be at Level 1 AND still contribute positively to CCRPI if they show growth.

AP Connection: Chronic absence, ODRs, and students failing courses all suppress progress by removing students from consistent learning time. Keeping students in class and engaged is the #1 growth lever.

Tool: Georgia uses SGPs (Student Growth Percentiles). A student in the 50th SGP is growing as expected.
⚖️ Achievement Gap Component 20% of Score
Measures the closing of gaps between highest- and lowest-performing subgroups. Subgroups include: Students with Disabilities, English Learners, Economically Disadvantaged, and racial/ethnic groups.

AP Connection: Your ODR disproportionality data, chronic absence by subgroup, and pathway access directly impact whether the gap is closing or growing. Review all metrics through a subgroup lens each week.
🎓 Ready Graduate Indicator
Georgia's signal that students are truly college or career ready — not just graduating. Requirements include:

• Pathway completion (CTE, AP, dual enrollment, military)
• Industry credential OR ACT/SAT benchmark OR dual enrollment credit
• On-time graduation (4-year cohort)

AP Connection: Your pathway % on the Student Data tab directly measures this. Griffin's goal should be 80%+ students enrolled in a recognized pathway.
📚 Literacy & ELA
Reading on grade level is the foundational skill for all GMAS subjects. A student who cannot read proficiently will struggle on every EOC — Algebra I word problems, Biology passages, US History documents.

AP Connection: The "% Reading on Grade Level" metric on your dashboard is an early warning system. Low ELA proficiency predicts low performance across all content areas. Prioritize literacy intervention for any student below grade-level Lexile.

One-Time Setup

Host on Google Sites — Everyone Gets Updates Automatically

✅ Once hosted on Google Sites, your team never needs to download a new HTML file again. When updates are made, just replace the file on Sites and everyone gets the new version automatically on next reload.
Step 1 — Open Google Sites

Go to sites.google.com and click + New Site. Name it something like Griffin AP Dashboard. You only need to create this once.

Step 2 — Embed the Dashboard

In the Google Sites editor, click Insert → Embed. Google Sites can embed an HTML file hosted on Google Drive. Here's how:

  1. Upload griffin_ap_dashboard.html to your Google Drive
  2. Right-click the file → Get link → set to Anyone with the link can view
  3. In Google Sites → Insert → Embed → paste the Drive link
  4. Resize the embed to fill the full page
  5. Click Publish in the top right
⚡Better option: Use the direct Google Drive preview URL format: https://drive.google.com/file/d/YOUR_FILE_ID/preview — paste that as the embed URL in Sites for a cleaner full-screen experience.
Step 3 — Share the Sites Link With Your Team

Once published, Google Sites gives you a URL like sites.google.com/view/griffin-ap-dashboard. Share that link with all APs. They bookmark it and that's all they ever need — no downloading files.

Step 4 — How to Update in the Future

When the dashboard is updated:

  1. Download the new griffin_ap_dashboard.html file
  2. Go to Google Drive → find the old file → right-click → Manage versions → Upload new version
  3. That's it — the Sites link automatically serves the new version
  4. Team members just reload their browser — no new link, no new download
Important — Google Sheet URL

When the dashboard is embedded in Google Sites, each person still needs to connect their Google Sheet URL the first time they open it. Once connected, the URL is stored in their browser and they won't need to do it again unless they clear their browser data.

Pro tip: To make the Sheet URL permanent so nobody ever has to paste it, open the HTML file in a text editor, find the line const SHEETS_URL = 'YOUR_APPS_SCRIPT_URL_HERE' and replace it with your actual URL before uploading to Drive. Then it's baked in for everyone automatically.
